// 01 — what this is

What is Nerd Lab

Nerd Lab is an open, experimental playground for people who like to explore technology in unconventional ways.

It exists for three things: knowledge creation, awareness building, and showing colleagues what these tools actually do on a normal working day — AI agents, RAG, automation, data, web and app building.

It is not a company. It is not a course. It is not a corporate AI initiative. It is a group of true nerds in the making, and the entrance requirement is curiosity.

// 02 — why

Why it exists

  1. 01

    Learn by building

    Nothing here starts with a curriculum. We take a problem that is mildly infuriating, overengineer a solution to it, and work out what we learned on the way back out.

  2. 02

    Make new tech approachable for the curious

    Most of this stops being intimidating the moment you watch someone else do it badly first. We do that part in public, on purpose.

  3. 03

    Open source everything

    Every experiment, including the ones that did not work. Especially those — the failure is usually the interesting half.

// 04 — join

Join the lab

You do not need an AI background. You do not need a data background. You do not need a technical background at all.

If you are the kind of person who opens the settings menu just to see what is in there, you are already qualified. Everything we build is open source, and people who keep showing up become co-maintainers — not as a reward, just as an accurate description of what has already happened.

Start in Discussions Ask the question you think is too basic. It is not. Browse the repositories Pick something up, or report that something is broken.
